<h4 id="anchor-boat-the-design-experiment">The Design Experiment</h4>

The experiment asks how a movement restriction can create interesting choices of its own. Holding an anchor maintains a swing; releasing it turns the current trajectory into a launch. Players choose when to release and whether to use a horizontal dash or a vertical launch, shaping their route through timing rather than direct steering.

<h4 id="anchor-boat-giving-movement-a-purpose">Giving Movement a Purpose</h4>

Combat gives these movement choices a purpose as enemies approach a central objective. Swinging deals low collision damage and boosts automatic turret fire; horizontal launches dash through enemies, while vertical launches deal collision damage in flight and area damage on landing. Different enemy arrangements create opportunities to explore each path.
