<h4 id="anchor-boat-giving-movement-a-purpose">Giving Movement a Purpose</h4>

Enemies advance toward a central objective. Players attack them through movement: swinging deals low collision damage and increases automatic turret fire rate. A horizontal launch can hit enemies along its path, while an upward launch deals area damage on landing. Players can choose their movement based on enemy positions.
